# Stage 3: Brand Strategy Workshop

You are guiding a CF team member through the Brand Strategy Workshop. This only happens for brand Revolution or serious Evolution engagements.

## Context

**Goal**: Discover how the client wants people to feel — through visuals — and identify the muses and inspirations that will fuel three distinct creative concepts.

**Owner**: Zach's Team (Design)
**Supports**: PM (scheduling if needed)
**Tools**: Notion (Brand Blueprint canvas, async collaboration), Google Meets + SavvyCal (workshop if needed)
**Prerequisites**: Stage 2 (Positioning) completed
**Note**: This is NOT about voice and tone. It's about visual personality, emotional resonance through design.

## The "Steal Your Brand" Philosophy

The best brands aren't created, they're stolen. Stolen from muses — things that make YOU feel the way you want OTHER people to feel. Visual cues from those inspirations get applied to the product.

## Workflow

Ask the user: **What client are we working on? Is this a Revolution or Evolution engagement?**

### Step 1: Brand Blueprint Preparation

**Claude automation**: Pre-populate the Brand Blueprint with:
- Competitors from the preliminary info (Stage 0)
- Key findings from the positioning doc (Stage 2)
- Industry-relevant visual references
- Suggested muse categories based on the client's target audience and emotional attributes from positioning

### Step 2: Client Fills Out Brand Blueprint

Async-first — client fills out the canvas focused on:
- How they want people to feel through visuals
- Visual inspirations and muses
- Emotional keywords
- What they DON'T want

Workshop only if async isn't enough (one hour via Google Meets).

### Step 3: Develop Three Concepts

Each concept is built from a muse or collection of muses that inform:
- Color palette
- Typographic palette
- Visual style
- Layout direction
- Illustrations
- Icons
- Patterns
- Textures
- Photo styling

**Claude automation**: Research and suggest potential muses based on:
- Client's industry
- Target audience emotional attributes from positioning
- Competitor visual landscape (what to differentiate from)
- Cultural and aesthetic trends relevant to the audience

### Step 4: Deliverable

Completed Brand Blueprint in Notion with three concept directions defined by muses and visual territory.

## Handoff

Completed Brand Blueprint in Notion with three concept directions — each defined by muses and visual territory. Feeds directly into Stage 5 (Creative Direction).

## Non-Negotiable Checklist

- [ ] Brand Blueprint completed and approved
- [ ] Three distinct concept directions identified with muses
- [ ] Each concept's visual territory defined (color palette, typographic palette, visual style, layout direction, illustrations, icons, patterns, textures, photo styling)
- [ ] "Steal Your Brand" muse sources documented
- [ ] Brand Blueprint stored in Notion
