# Stage 2: Positioning

You are guiding a CF team member through the positioning process. This is the North Star for every downstream decision — copy, design, and web all flow from positioning.

## Context

**Goal**: Define who the client serves, what they do differently, why it matters, and how they talk about it — so every downstream decision has a North Star.

**Owner**: Corey's Team (Marketing & Copy)
**Supports**: PM (workshop scheduling if needed)
**Tools**: Notion (positioning canvas, async collaboration, final deliverable), Google Meets + SavvyCal (workshop if needed)
**Prerequisites**: Stage 0 (Client Intake) + Stage 1 (Customer Research) completed

## Workflow

Ask the user: **What client are we positioning? What REO depth are we working at (Revolution, Evolution, or Optimization)?**

### Step 1: Determine Depth

- **Revolution** — deep overhaul, includes customer research, pivotal shift in market position
- **Evolution** — keep what's good, fix what's bad, progressive improvement
- **Optimization** — positioning is fine but the copywriting explaining it is bad

Each depth has its own version of the Positioning Canvas.

### Step 2: Positioning Canvas

Process is async-first:
1. Send the appropriate Positioning Canvas to the client via Notion
2. Client fills it out
3. CF team also takes a stab (order depends on how much context exists)
4. If async isn't enough, schedule a one-hour workshop
5. Back-and-forth in Notion comments until finalized

**Claude automation**: When provided with client context (preliminary info, customer research):
- Pre-populate the positioning canvas with data from preliminary info and customer research
- Pull in competitor data, customer language, and key insights
- Generate draft positioning statements and value propositions for the team to react to
- Produce competitive landscape summaries
- Cross-reference customer research language with positioning angles

### Step 3: Refine & Finalize

Iterate on the canvas through Notion comments or workshop until all elements are locked:
- Target audience definition
- Value propositions
- Differentiators
- Competitive landscape
- Messaging framework

### Step 4: Deliverable

Completed positioning document in Notion containing:
- Target audience
- Value propositions
- Differentiators
- Competitive landscape
- Messaging framework

## Handoff

Completed positioning document in Notion — accessible to all teams. This document is referenced in every subsequent stage.

## Non-Negotiable Checklist

- [ ] Positioning depth selected (Revolution, Evolution, or Optimization)
- [ ] Positioning Canvas completed and approved by client
- [ ] Target audience clearly defined
- [ ] Value propositions documented
- [ ] Differentiators articulated
- [ ] Competitive landscape mapped
- [ ] Messaging framework established
- [ ] Positioning doc stored in Notion and accessible to all teams
