### What counts and what doesn't

What you have doesn't qualify as customer research:

- **A/B test data** — performance validation, not customer insight
- **Testimonials/case studies** — social proof, not research
- **Performance Model tool** — data collection for sales modeling, not customer understanding

The distinction matters: you know your product works (A/B data proves it), but there's no evidence you've done the work to understand *why* customers switch, what language resonates, what objections exist, or who the buyer actually is psychologically.

## Keyword Research

Your SEO authority is strong — DR 60, outranking most direct competitors on organic traffic. The problem isn't authority, it's targeting. Almost everything driving your traffic today is generic developer content that your actual buyer (e.g. a VP of Marketing or Head of Ecommerce at a scaling DTC brand) never searches for.

The keyword research below is organized by the three products and the content gaps identified in this audit. Total search volume in this category is low by design — this is an enterprise B2B product. The opportunity is to own every buyer-intent keyword before a competitor or third party does.

### What you're ranking for now (and why it's the wrong traffic)

Your top traffic drivers today: "mobile SEO checklist," "mobile first indexing," "edge SEO," "total blocking time," "crawl budget." These are developer and SEO practitioner searches. Your ideal customer’s are less likely to be arriving from any of them. Two of your top 25 keywords are branded — meaning almost all non-brand traffic is arriving from people who may not buy.

### What not to target

Competitor comparison keywords like "cloudflare alternative" and "fastly alternative" exist with some volume, but those audiences are less valuable — they're general infrastructure buyers, not ecommerce operators. Positioning against Cloudflare or Fastly risks credibility unless the framing is explicitly "ecom-specialized vs. general infrastructure." 

Nostra vs. Edgemesh and Nostra vs. Yottaa comparison pages are a better play — no one is ranking for those terms and the audience match is exact.

### The content gap underneath all of this

None of these keyword opportunities require you to create content you don't already have the material for. Your case studies contain the performance data. Your expert guides have the authority. Your product pages have the technical depth. 

The gap is pulling that material into SEO-optimized, properly structured pages that are actually visible to Google and connected by internal links. Fix the expert guides (currently broken), gate the high-intent tools (ROI calculator, speed test), and build the three product-specific content hubs above.
