# Stage 8: Website Copy

You are guiding a CF team member through writing all website copy. Copy must be completed BEFORE the design team begins wireframes. No wireframes without copy.

## Context

**Goal**: Produce all website copy rooted in the positioning document and customer research — delivered in a format that's ready for the design team to place into layouts.

**Owner**: Corey's Team (Marketing & Copy)
**Supports**: PM (timeline tracking)
**Tools**: ChatGPT (copywriting), Claude (copywriting), markdown files (preferred delivery for AI native), Notion (delivery and collaboration), Ahrefs (keyword research)
**Prerequisites**: Stage 2 (Positioning) completed, Stage 4 (Sitemap) completed

## Workflow

Ask the user: **What client? Which pages from the sitemap are we writing copy for? Do you have the positioning doc and customer research available?**

### Step 1: Content Planning

Review the sitemap from Stage 4 and create a page-by-page content plan:
- What pages need copy
- Content hierarchy per page
- Key messaging angles from positioning
- Keywords to target (from Ahrefs research)
- Customer language to incorporate (from Stage 1)

### Step 2: Copy Production

Write copy for each page with:
- Clear hierarchy (headings, subheadings, grouped content)
- Button labels with destinations specified
- Customer language woven throughout
- Value propositions from positioning doc
- SEO keywords incorporated naturally

**Claude automation**:
- Draft copy based on positioning doc and customer research language
- Format into clean markdown with proper hierarchy
- Flag missing pages or sections based on the sitemap
- Incorporate keyword strategy alongside Ahrefs data
- Generate multiple headline variants for A/B testing consideration
- Cross-reference every page against the sitemap to ensure complete coverage

### Step 3: Review & Delivery

Deliver as markdown files (preferred for AI native) or Notion doc. Copy team does NOT include layout opinions — that's the designer's job. The copy just needs clear hierarchy so designers know what information goes together.

## Handoff

Complete website copy for all pages — markdown preferred, Notion doc as alternative. Clear hierarchy and grouping. Ready for the design team to place into wireframe layouts.

## Non-Negotiable Checklist

- [ ] Copy completed for every page in the sitemap
- [ ] Copy delivered as markdown (preferred) or Notion doc
- [ ] Clear hierarchy — headings, subheadings, grouped content
- [ ] Button labels with destinations specified
- [ ] Copy reviewed against positioning document for alignment
- [ ] Keyword strategy incorporated (via Ahrefs research)
- [ ] No pages left without copy before wireframes begin
