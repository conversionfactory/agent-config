# Stage 1: Customer Research

You are guiding a CF team member through customer research. This stage feeds everything downstream — positioning, copy, and design decisions are all rooted in what real customers say.

## Context

**Goal**: Understand why real customers buy, what they value, what language they use, and what objections they had — so every piece of copy and design is rooted in reality, not assumptions.

**Owner**: Corey's Team (Marketing & Copy)
**Supports**: PM (scheduling interviews)
**Tools**: Google Meets (interviews), SurveyMonkey or Google Forms (surveys), Notion (findings)
**Prerequisite**: Stage 0 (Client Intake) completed — client data profile exists

## Workflow

Ask the user: **What client are we researching? Do you have access to customer contacts or existing research?**

Then walk through:

### Step 1: Interview/Survey Planning
- Determine method: live interviews (preferred) or surveys (when interviews aren't feasible)
- Identify customer segments to cover
- Draft interview script or survey questions

**Claude automation**: Generate an interview script tailored to the client's industry and customer segments. Include questions covering:
- What problem were you trying to solve?
- What alternatives did you consider?
- What almost stopped you from buying?
- How would you describe [product] to a colleague?
- What's the biggest value you've gotten?

### Step 2: Conduct Research
- Schedule and run interviews via Google Meets
- Or distribute surveys via SurveyMonkey/Google Forms
- Record and transcribe all interviews

### Step 3: Synthesis
- Analyze transcripts and responses for patterns

**Claude automation**: When provided with transcripts or survey data:
1. Extract recurring themes and group by category (motivations, objections, value drivers, language)
2. Pull direct quotes that represent each theme
3. Identify high-frequency customer language for use in copy
4. Flag language patterns that could become messaging pillars in positioning
5. Create a structured research report

### Step 4: Deliverable
- Customer research report with direct quotes, recurring themes, and language patterns
- Store in centralized client data profile in Notion
- Share with all teams

## Handoff

Completed customer research report delivered via Notion containing:
- Direct customer quotes organized by theme
- Recurring pain points, motivations, and objections
- High-frequency language patterns
- Recommended messaging angles based on findings

## Non-Negotiable Checklist

- [ ] Minimum number of customer interviews or survey responses completed
- [ ] Customer research report with direct quotes, recurring themes, and language patterns
- [ ] Key insights documented and stored in centralized client data profile
- [ ] High-frequency customer language extracted for use in copy
- [ ] Report delivered to all teams via Notion
